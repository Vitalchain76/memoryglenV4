**Mwari Mubatsiri Wedu** — full text, verbatim from the booklet:

> Mwari mubatsiri wedu
> Dakara nazvino,
> Mudziviriri munhamo
> Nebanza narini.
> Zvese zvisati zvasikwa
> Makomo nepasi
> Imi makanga muripo
> Mugari narini.
>
> Kuneni misi mizhinji
> Inenge kanguva
> Senguva yemangwanani
> Kusati kwayedza
> Nhambwe dzinomburumbuka
> Nemisi yesezve.
> Zvinokanganika zvese
> Sekurota hope.

This is the Shona setting of **"O God, Our Help in Ages Past"** — Isaac Watts, 1719, from Psalm 90. It is one of the oldest hymns in the English-speaking church and it is public domain, so we can print the corresponding English freely:

> O God, our help in ages past,
> Our hope for years to come,
> Our shelter from the stormy blast,
> And our eternal home.
>
> A thousand ages in Thy sight
> Are like an evening gone;
> Short as the watch that ends the night
> Before the rising sun.

Proposal: Shona first and largest, English beneath in italic, with a line noting it is Psalm 90.
