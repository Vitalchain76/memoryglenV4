You have written these as **your** relations — your grandfather, your uncle. On
your mother's memorial they would read as **her** relations, which are different:
Chiimba is her married name, so Chari would be her **father-in-law** and Moses her
**brother-in-law**.

1. **Relative to Virginia** — "Her father-in-law", "Her brother-in-law"
2. **Relative to the family** — "Grandfather", "Uncle", with a line saying whose
3. **Both** — as we did with Sekuru and Amaini: family term first, relation to
   Virginia beneath

I'd suggest **3**, since it worked well for the Mushore side. But this is your
family's language and I won't choose it for you.
